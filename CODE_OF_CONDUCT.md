# Code of conduct

EyeCash wants to be a friendly place for anyone interested in personal finance and privacy, whatever their experience.

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or unwanted personal attention.
- Respect privacy: never post someone else's personal or financial data, and do not ask others for theirs.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block participants who do not follow these rules. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through GitHub ([@CrisBilli](https://github.com/CrisBilli)).
